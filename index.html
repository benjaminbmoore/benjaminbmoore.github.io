01 Profile

I help growth organisations get bigger without losing their way. New markets. New products. New partners.

I’m an executive leader in growth organisations. As Chief Strategy Officer at JNCTN, a growth-stage SaaS company founded in Wellington, I set the company’s direction, lead international expansion, and work with investors and the Board as we scale.

My work spans highly regulated industries, from business platforms to public services and consumer products. I’ve seen how each one works from the inside, through my roles in consulting, architecture and product.
